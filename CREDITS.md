# Curriculum provenance and references

The supplied `Curriculum Outline Example.docx` contains a screenshot titled "Possible Kodaly-Inspired Curriculum Outline," attributed to Victoria Boler. The screenshot covers K to 5 in Melody, Rhythm, Form, Texture, Expression, and Other. This app uses that sequence as a starting point, with original definitions and instructional drafts. The original screenshot and document are not bundled.

- Victoria Boler, curriculum outline context: https://www.victoriaboler.com/blog/3-curriculum-outlines
- Indiana Department of Education, 2023 Music Standards: https://www.in.gov/doe/files/IAS-Grades-K-12-Music.pdf
- Open Music Theory, fundamentals reference: https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/
- Gordon Institute for Music Learning, audiation: https://giml.org/mlt/audiation/
- Ball State University, music education program: https://www.bsu.edu/academics/collegesanddepartments/music/degrees-and-programs/undergraduate-majors/music-education

Grade 6 entries are proposed extensions, not items from the uploaded image. The source wording "dotted quarter triplet" was separated into dotted quarter and triplet with an instructor-review note. Grade placements are not Indiana-mandated grade-by-grade requirements. Faculty should verify definitions, readiness, and standards alignment before course adoption.

The Maestro Cat character sheets were supplied by the project owner. The pose images in `assets/cats/` are background-removed crops of those sheets. This package makes no additional license claim for supplied artwork. Audio demonstrations are generated with browser oscillators; no commercial recordings are bundled.
