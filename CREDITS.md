# Curriculum provenance and references

The supplied `Curriculum Outline Example.docx` contains a screenshot titled “Possible Kodaly-Inspired Curriculum Outline,” attributed to Victoria Boler. The screenshot covers K–5 in Melody, Rhythm, Form, Texture, Expression, and Other. This app uses that sequence as a starting point, with original definitions and instructional drafts. The original screenshot/document is not bundled in this distribution.

- Victoria Boler, curriculum outline context: https://www.victoriaboler.com/blog/3-curriculum-outlines
- Indiana Department of Education, 2023 Music Standards: https://www.in.gov/doe/files/IAS-Grades-K-12-Music.pdf
- Open Music Theory, fundamentals reference: https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/
- Gordon Institute for Music Learning, audiation: https://giml.org/mlt/audiation/
- Ball State University, music education program: https://www.bsu.edu/academics/collegesanddepartments/music/degrees-and-programs/undergraduate-majors/music-education

Grade 6 entries are proposed extensions, not items from the uploaded image. The source wording “dotted quarter triplet” was separated into dotted quarter and triplet with an instructor-review note. Grade placements are not Indiana-mandated grade-by-grade requirements. Faculty should verify definitions, readiness, and task-level standards alignment before course adoption.

The Maestro Cat character reference was supplied by the project owner. This package does not make any additional license claim for supplied artwork or third-party reference materials. Website audio demonstrations are generated with browser oscillators; no commercial music recordings are bundled.
